Three vehicles LEDs (RED, YELLOW, and GREEN)
Two pedestrian LEDs (RED and GREEN)
PB switch used to indicate that a pedestrian has requested to cross the street


System -> ON, The vehicle GREEN LED -> ON, and the pedestrian RED LED -> ON
The vehicle GREEN LED will stay ON until the PB switch is pressed OR it has been 20 seconds since the vehicle GREEN LED was last turned ON.


Once one of these two conditions is met, the following actions happen:


After 2 seconds: the vehicle GREEN LED turns OFF and the vehicle YELLOW LED turns ON
After 2 seconds: the vehicle RED LED turns ON and the vehicle YELLOW LED turns OFF


After 1 second: the pedestrian GREEN LED turns ON for 5 seconds, then it flashes for 4 seconds with a frequency of 0.25 seconds


The pedestrian GREEN LED turns OFF and the pedestrian RED LED turns ON


After 1 second, the vehicle GREEN LED turns ON again and the vehicle RED LED turns OFF.


The system resets to its initial state and waits till the PB switch is pressed and it cycles again as described above.
